# Candidate83: one cumulative post-partial risk envelope

Issue1263 authorizes one successor of exact PR1262 CAPREUSE. All original reg71 signals, entry filters, close→next-open execution, rational cap1, profit-only D3 third partial, native floor, pre-partial momentum, post-partial BE/SMA10 and time20 replacement remain inherited. This is ZEL_POST_PARTIAL_RISK_ENVELOPE, not a new trader-original rule. No year/symbol/trade-ID exception, parameter/threshold/source sweep or C63 replay.

Groups are contiguous same-symbol occupancy episodes. A root actual entry creates a group; subsequent actually filled reuse entries join while it remains occupied. The group closes/resets only after actual fills leave zero quantity. Realized cash of exited group members remains in the group's realized ledger until that reset, consistent with existing closed-plus-marked accounting. Remaining active quantities alone receive hypothetical liquidation marks. Dropping a closed member's realized cash would manufacture a giveback without an economic loss; no such transfer-loss is booked. No cash, bank or peak carries into a fresh root group.

At every completed native4h close, after earlier observable open fills, compute group realized net plus residual close-mark net with full frozen costs/funding. Only source partial legs actually filled strictly before that close timestamp contribute max(0,normalized partial net) to bank. A source profit-decision partial may gap to a loss; that loss never funds bank. The first actual source partial starts causal peak observations; zero bank leaves risk inactive. Peak uses completed closes only, not intra-bar highs, a future daily close, terminal winner labels or equal-time unexecuted opens. Current mark updates peak before evaluating group_net <= peak - positive_bank. No added numerical risk threshold.

On the first trigger, next observable open closes all remaining group members. Already-due source full exits, especially native floor, remain their original single final fill. A due source partial still fills its original third; the wrapper closes its remaining quantity at that same open, with quantity-weighted costs and no double whole-position charge. No source exit is delayed. Pending boundary exits are not filled; residual mark plus costs/funding is retained.

Entry decisions keep PR1262's conservative phase: reserve available quantity at signal close before equal-timestamp open fills. Actual old fills then occur, followed by any reserved original entry subject to original gap/window safety. Risk decision does not free capacity, cancel an otherwise valid signal or upsize its reservation. If the old group becomes flat at that open, an already reserved entry starts a fresh group at its reserved size. A later ordinary signal can use the fully available cap. Minimum quantity remains the native strictly positive exact rational research quantity; exchange lot/notional compatibility is not claimed.

Implementation materializes the unchanged unit source path once for each admitted child signal, then merges chronological observations and risk truncations. Source references are retained for independent saved prefix checks. Only leg fills strictly earlier than the close and current completed prices enter execution decisions; cached later source legs/exit outcomes never select or size entries. References beyond a wrapper exit are diagnostic source continuations, not actual child trades or extra parent economic replays.

Accounting is the exact PR1262 normalized leg adapter. One actual signal entry creates one WR campaign; partials never add campaigns. Net/cost2/exposure multiply unit legs by allocated reference quantity. Risk wrappers can change later occupancy/campaigns; all common/new/excluded and closed↔open effects enter the exact PnL bridge. Raw MFE/MAE remain underlying unscaled-path diagnostics.

Saved DD attribution uses the inherited UTC after-open reporting convention, with terminal close and no excluded future open. Risk decisions use completed4h close observations before next-open fills. These are intentionally distinct existing execution/reporting phases. Own peak/trough/recovery windows are reconstructed. Adjacent DD excess equals child-window same-calendar effect plus parent-window relocation; different maximum-DD windows are never presented as one causal subtraction. Fixed leg-role partitions (original, source partial, source remainder, reuse) and realized/unrealized cash movements reconcile exactly. Cash transfers between marked and realized buckets are not independently counted as losses. These are descriptive saved-only diagnostics and do not choose the rule.
